\section{The Standard Model}
\label{sec:INTRODUCTION_THE_STANDARD_MODEL}
\pcstart{s12}{2}

The \gls{sm} of particle physics is a \gls{qft} that describes three of the four known fundamental forces (electromagnetic, weak, and strong interactions) and classifies all known elementary particles \cite{standardmodel}.

\subsection{Fermions}
\label{sec:INTRODUCTION_THE_STANDARD_MODEL_FERMIONS}

Fermions are the matter particles, divided into:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Quarks: up, down, charm, strange, top~\cite{top-discovery}, bottom
  \item Leptons: electron, muon, tau, and their corresponding neutrinos
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Bosons}
\label{sec:INTRODUCTION_THE_STANDARD_MODEL_BOSONS}

Bosons are the force carriers:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Photon ($\gamma$) - electromagnetic force
  \item W and Z bosons - weak force~\cite{w-boson,z-boson}
  \item Gluons - strong force
  \item Higgs boson - mass generation~\cite{higgs1964,higgs-discovery,cms-higgs}
\end{itemize}

\pcend{s12}
