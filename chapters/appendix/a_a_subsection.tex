\subsection{Cross-Section Formula}
\label{sec:APPENDIX_DERIVATIONS_CROSS_SECTION}

The differential cross section of a $2 \to 2$ scattering process in the centre-of-mass frame is
\begin{equation}
  \frac{d\sigma}{d\Omega} = \frac{1}{64\pi^2 s}\left|\frac{p_f}{p_i}\right||\mathcal{M}|^2,
  \label{eq:APPENDIX_CROSS_SECTION}
\end{equation}
where $s$ is the Mandelstam variable, $p_i$ and $p_f$ are the initial and final momenta, and $\mathcal{M}$ is the matrix element.
