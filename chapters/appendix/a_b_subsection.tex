\subsection{Feynman Rules}
\label{sec:APPENDIX_DERIVATIONS_FEYNMAN_RULES}

The Feynman rules of \gls{qed} are:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Photon propagator:} $\dfrac{-ig_{\mu\nu}}{q^2 + i\epsilon}$
  \item \textbf{Fermion propagator:} $\dfrac{i(\gamma^\mu p_\mu + m)}{p^2 - m^2 + i\epsilon}$
  \item \textbf{Vertex:} $-ie\gamma^\mu$
\end{itemize}
