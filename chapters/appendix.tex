\section{Mathematical Derivations}

\subsection{Derivation of Cross Section Formula}

The differential cross section for a $2 \to 2$ scattering process is given by:
\begin{equation}
  \frac{d\sigma}{d\Omega} = \frac{1}{64\pi^2 s}\left|\frac{p_f}{p_i}\right||\mathcal{M}|^2
  \label{eq:cross-section-formula}
\end{equation}

where $s$ is the Mandelstam variable, $p_i$ and $p_f$ are initial and final momenta, and $\mathcal{M}$ is the matrix element.

\subsection{Feynman Rules}

The Feynman rules for QED interactions:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Photon propagator: $\frac{-ig_{\mu\nu}}{q^2 + i\epsilon}$
  \item Fermion propagator: $\frac{i(\gamma^\mu p_\mu + m)}{p^2 - m^2 + i\epsilon}$
  \item Vertex: $-ie\gamma^\mu$
\end{itemize}
