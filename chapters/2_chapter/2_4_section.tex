\section{Quantum Chromodynamics}
\label{sec:THEORY_QUANTUM_CHROMODYNAMICS}
\pcstart{s24}{2}

\Gls{qcd} describes the strong interaction between quarks and gluons. Key features include:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Asymptotic freedom at high energies~\cite{qcd-asymptotic-freedom,politzer-qcd}
  \item Confinement at low energies
  \item Running of the strong coupling constant $\alpha_s$
\end{itemize}
