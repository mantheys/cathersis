\section{Quantum Field Theory}
\label{sec:THEORY_QUANTUM_FIELD_THEORY}
\pcstart{s21}{2}

\Gls{qft} combines quantum mechanics with special relativity. In \gls{qft}, particles are excitations of underlying quantum fields.

\subsection{Lagrangian Formalism}
\label{sec:THEORY_QUANTUM_FIELD_THEORY_LAGRANGIAN_FORMALISM}

The dynamics of a quantum field are described by a Lagrangian density $\mathcal{L}$. For a scalar field $\phi$, the Klein-Gordon Lagrangian is:
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{L} = \frac{1}{2}\partial_\mu\phi\partial^\mu\phi - \frac{1}{2}m^2\phi^2
  \label{eq:klein-gordon}
\end{equation}
