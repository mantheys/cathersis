\section{Electroweak Theory}
\label{sec:THEORY_ELECTROWEAK_THEORY}
\pcstart{s23}{2}

The electromagnetic and weak forces are unified in the electroweak theory~\cite{standardmodel,weinberg-model,salam-ward}. The weak mixing angle $\theta_W$ relates the coupling constants:
\begin{equation}
  \sin^2\theta_W = 1 - \frac{M_W^2}{M_Z^2}
  \label{eq:weinberg-angle}
\end{equation}

where $M_W$ and $M_Z$ are the masses of the W and Z bosons.

\pcend{s23}
