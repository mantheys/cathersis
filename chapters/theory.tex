\chapter{Theoretical Framework}
\label{ch:theory}

This chapter presents the theoretical foundations underlying this research, including the relevant quantum field theory and specific models.

\section{Quantum Field Theory}

Quantum Field Theory (QFT) combines quantum mechanics with special relativity. In QFT, particles are excitations of underlying quantum fields.

\subsection{Lagrangian Formalism}

The dynamics of a quantum field are described by a Lagrangian density $\mathcal{L}$. For a scalar field $\phi$, the Klein-Gordon Lagrangian is:
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{L} = \frac{1}{2}\partial_\mu\phi\partial^\mu\phi - \frac{1}{2}m^2\phi^2
  \label{eq:klein-gordon}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Feynman Diagrams}

Particle interactions can be represented using Feynman diagrams, which provide a pictorial representation of mathematical expressions describing particle interactions.

\section{The Standard Model Lagrangian}

The Standard Model Lagrangian can be written as:
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{L}_{SM} = \mathcal{L}_{gauge} + \mathcal{L}_{fermion} + \mathcal{L}_{Higgs} + \mathcal{L}_{Yukawa}
  \label{eq:sm-lagrangian}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Gauge Symmetries}

The Standard Model is based on the gauge group $SU(3)_C \times SU(2)_L \times U(1)_Y$, representing:
\begin{itemize}
  \item $SU(3)_C$ - color symmetry (strong force)
  \item $SU(2)_L$ - weak isospin symmetry
  \item $U(1)_Y$ - hypercharge symmetry
\end{itemize}

\section{Electroweak Theory}

The electromagnetic and weak forces are unified in the electroweak theory. The weak mixing angle $\theta_W$ relates the coupling constants:
\begin{equation}
  \sin^2\theta_W = 1 - \frac{M_W^2}{M_Z^2}
  \label{eq:weinberg-angle}
\end{equation}

where $M_W$ and $M_Z$ are the masses of the W and Z bosons.

\section{Quantum Chromodynamics}

Quantum Chromodynamics (QCD) describes the strong interaction between quarks and gluons. Key features include:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Asymptotic freedom at high energies
  \item Confinement at low energies
  \item Running of the strong coupling constant $\alpha_s$
\end{itemize}
